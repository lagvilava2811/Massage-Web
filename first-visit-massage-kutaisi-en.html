Before your first visit: what to tell your massage therapist

A short conversation before a first visit helps agree the right time and format.

Useful information

  • What concerns you and what you would like to improve;
  • Your usual daily activity;
  • A preferred time and 30- or 45-minute format;
  • For a child’s visit, the child’s age.

Why agreement matters

Session duration and method depend on age, condition and needs. The conversation helps make sure the visit fits your goal.

When to speak with a doctor first

For acute or increasing pain, a new injury, numbness, weakness or another concerning symptom, contact a doctor first.
