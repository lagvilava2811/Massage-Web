Therapeutic massage in Kutaisi: planning an individual session

Each visit starts with a conversation about your goals and a suitable format.

People may book a therapeutic massage to discuss body care, movement-related discomfort or support during a recovery process. Each situation is individual.

Before booking

The first conversation helps discuss your goal, convenient time and whether the visit is suitable. Sessions are available in 30- and 45-minute formats.

Important

For acute or increasing pain, a new injury, numbness, weakness or another concerning symptom, contact a doctor first.
