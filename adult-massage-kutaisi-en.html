Massage for adults in Kutaisi: when and why people book

A visit is planned individually, taking into account daily activity, concerns and goals.

Adults often consider massage during periods of daily workload, long sitting, physical work or stress. Tension in the neck, shoulders and back is a familiar feeling for many people.

Before the first visit

A short conversation helps discuss your needs and agree a suitable format. Sessions of 30 or 45 minutes are available; method and duration are agreed in advance.

When to speak with a doctor first

For acute or increasing pain, a new injury, numbness, weakness or another concerning symptom, contact a doctor first.

Would you like to book?

Time and suitability are agreed with Nino in advance.
