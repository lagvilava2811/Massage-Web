Rehabilitation in Kutaisi: planning your visit

The goal of a rehabilitation visit differs from person to person.

During the first conversation, we discuss daily movement, your goal and a convenient schedule. When booking, briefly describe what concerns you and what you would like to improve.

Individual format

Session duration and method depend on age, condition and needs. The times on the site are a guide; the particular visit is agreed in advance.

When to speak with a doctor first

For acute or increasing pain, a new injury, numbness, weakness or another concerning symptom, contact a doctor first.
