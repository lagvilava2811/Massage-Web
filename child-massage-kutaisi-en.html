Children's massage in Kutaisi: what parents should know

Planning begins with a calm conversation about the child’s needs and the parent’s questions.

When booking, it is useful to share the child’s age, the purpose of the visit and any information you consider important. This helps discuss a suitable format and time in advance.

Planning the visit

Nino discusses the child’s age, daily routine, purpose of the visit and comfort with the parent. The method and length are individual.

Before booking

  • Child’s age;
  • Purpose of the visit;
  • Useful information about the daily routine;
  • A convenient day and time.

When to speak with a pediatrician first

For acute pain, a new injury, fever or another concerning symptom, contact a pediatrician first. Suitability is agreed in advance.

A 30-minute children’s session costs 35 GEL; 45 minutes costs 50 GEL.

Would you like to book?

The time and suitability of the visit are agreed with Nino in advance.
