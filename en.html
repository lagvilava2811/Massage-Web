Planning your visit

Which service is right for your needs?

When booking, tell us about your concern and what you hope to achieve. This helps us discuss the service and the details of your first visit.

Questions

Before your visit

How do I arrange an appointment?

Call +995 571 088 021 or contact us on WhatsApp. Your visit is confirmed after agreeing a time with Nino.

Where is the practice?

Lomonosov 2, Balakhvani, Kutaisi. Select the address in the contact section to open Google Maps.

How much is a session and how long does it last?

For adults, 30 minutes costs GEL 40 and 45 minutes costs GEL 60. Pediatric massage: 30 minutes costs GEL 35 and 45 minutes costs GEL 50. Session length depends on the patient's condition and needs.

How should I prepare for my first visit?

When booking, we discuss your needs and explain what to consider or bring to your first visit.

Can I arrange pediatric massage?

Yes. Please share your child’s age and the purpose of the visit so we can discuss suitability and arrangements beforehand.

How can I reschedule?

If you cannot attend, contact us as early as possible by phone or WhatsApp to arrange another time.
